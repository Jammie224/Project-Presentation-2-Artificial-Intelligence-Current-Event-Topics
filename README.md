## Topic summary
AI is creating major economic value and boosting productivity, but it also brings job disruption, inequality risks, and new security challenges such as autonomous weapons, surveillance, and misinformation. These impacts show the need for ethical, human-centered development that ensures fairness, transparency, and accountability. Responsible regulation and worker reskilling will be essential to balance innovation with social protection.
