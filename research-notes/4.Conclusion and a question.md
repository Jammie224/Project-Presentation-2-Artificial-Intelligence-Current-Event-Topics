# Conclusion
AI is reshaping economies, jobs, and security systems worldwide. It brings major productivity gains and creates significant economic value, yet also risks job disruption, inequality, and misuse in warfare or surveillance. To ensure AI benefits society, ethical, transparent, and human-centered development is essential. Balancing innovation with safeguards—such as reskilling workers and regulating high-risk AI—will determine whether AI becomes a tool for progress or a source of harm.
